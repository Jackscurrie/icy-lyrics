# Icy Lyrics for iPhone end-user terms — draft

**Review required. Prepared September 4, 2026; no effective date assigned.**
This is proposed text for an agreement between the user and Jackscurrie, the
Icy Lyrics maintainer. It is not currently presented for acceptance in the
app. Reading, downloading or checking in this draft does not record consent,
establish enforceability, or constitute approval from Spotify or an upstream
copyright holder.

## Application and independent licenses

Icy Lyrics displays lyrics and controls playback through the installed
Spotify application. It is independently maintained and is not made,
endorsed or supported by Spotify. No guarantee is made that a provider will
continue to supply lyrics or playback access. This development build still
requires physical iPhone installation, refresh and playback testing. Keep
copies of your original imported TTML files.

The project's source license and each dependency's license continue to govern
their respective material. These proposed terms do not remove rights to use,
study, modify or distribute open-source code under its applicable license.
See the [project license](../../LICENSE) and bundled third-party notices.
The Spotify provisions below concern Spotify's platform, service and content;
they do not extend those restrictions to independently licensed Icy Lyrics
source, fonts or other components. This distinction does not resolve the
separate SDK/AGPL distribution issue identified in the
[distribution review](DISTRIBUTION-REVIEW.md).

4. Spotify is a third-party beneficiary of this agreement and its accompanying

These proposed clauses address the five minimum provisions of
[Spotify Developer Terms section V.11](https://developer.spotify.com/terms),
version 10 effective May 15, 2025, reviewed September 4, 2026. Mandatory legal
rights remain unaffected. This draft does not import Spotify's
developer-to-developer arbitration, indemnity or other obligations into the
app's user agreement.

## Privacy and external services

The [iPhone privacy draft](PRIVACY.md) describes current data handling, optional
credential sharing, storage and known deletion limits. It requires review
and publication before adoption. Spotify authorization and any experimental
provider consent are separate choices; accepting application terms does not
authorize extra scopes or token sharing. Spotify and other services also
apply their own terms to their services. Users should import only lyrics
they have permission to use.

For questions about Icy Lyrics or these proposed terms, contact
[jack@jackscurrie.com](mailto:jack@jackscurrie.com).
